table  (--size 1152x2048) -> tools/art/chrome.py -> public/art/table.webp
Match exactly the look of the reference: the same cold, desaturated blue-grey winter-dusk tabletop, the same warm amber candle lanterns, the same snowy pine branches with red holly berries, the same soft painterly-realistic rendering and lighting. Draw ONLY the empty table as a portrait background for a mobile game: remove every picture card, every card stack, the top bar, the gear and the round buttons, and fill where they were with plain table. Keep a lantern with a lit candle in the top-left corner and another in the top-right corner, partly cut by the edges, casting soft warm amber light onto the table below them. Snowy pine branches with red berries and drifts of snow and frost along the left, right and bottom edges, and a few out-of-focus snowflakes drifting near the edges. The whole middle of the table, from just below the lanterns to just above the bottom edge and across most of the width, is calm, smooth, dark slate blue-grey with only a very faint stone texture and a soft vignette, darker than the edges and completely empty: no objects, no snow, no patterns, no text. Seen from above at the same slight angle as the reference, deep focus, portrait 9:16.

stack-back  (--size 1024x1024 --background transparent) -> tools/art/chrome.py -> public/art/stack-back.webp

Draw ONLY one single card back, seen perfectly straight on from directly above, filling the frame and centred: a square with generously rounded corners, its face a deep blue-grey (#3a4759) with a fine frosted, slightly sparkly texture like frost on slate, a thin soft pale frosted highlight along its top and left rim and a slightly darker rim along the bottom and right, and exactly one small round warm amber dot (#e99645) in the very centre with a faint glow. No stack, no other cards, no shadow, no table, no text. Isolated on a transparent background.
