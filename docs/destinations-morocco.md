# Morocco: candidate destinations

Trip 14, boards 196–210 (creator, 2026-11-07). It moved back one trip on 2026-11-07, when East Coast America became trip 11. The trip adds no new art except its postcards
and its stamp: every everyday picture comes from the library. Each board has one real place,
shown as a postcard the size of the whole board. A 2×2 part of it, the **crop**, plays on the
board. How postcards are made is in `docs/picture-direction.md` ("Destinations").

**Shapes** (Levels, `docs/difficulty-curve.md`, "Trips 11-15 (plan)"):
- Most boards are 4×5, with a tall 3:5 postcard.
- 200 and 205 are 5×5, with a 3:4 postcard. The finale, 210, is 4×5.
- 201 is the all-long board: a 4×4 postcard that plays as a two-card strip.

**Tags.** *famous* is a place everyone knows. *hidden gem* is a place few players will know
but they'll want to visit. *silly* is something funny or odd. The mix is 8 famous, 4 gems and
3 silly. Marrakech has three (the Bahia, the Menara and El Badi) and Fes two.

**The rights rules, first** (checked 2026-11-07).
- **Morocco's freedom of panorama is doubtful.** Law No. 2-00 has a public-places exception,
  but Wikimedia Commons rates Morocco as having none for buildings and art. So every building on
  these postcards is centuries old and out of copyright:
  - Volubilis is Roman.
  - Aït Benhaddou, the medinas of Chefchaouen and Fes, Essaouira's Skala and the tanneries are
    old towns and works.
  - El Badi is from 1578, the Menara pavilion from 1869 and the Bahia from the 1860s–1900.
  - Nothing modern appears.
- **No mosques, shrines or holy towns.** That rules out the Hassan II Mosque, the Koutoubia,
  the Kairaouine, Moulay Idriss, the zaouias, and the Hassan Tower with Mohammed V's mausoleum.
  No minaret appears on any postcard.
- **No royal palaces or royal symbols.** That rules out the Dar al-Makhzen and its golden doors
  in Fes, the Rabat palace, the king's portrait, crowns and flags.
- **No licensed or modern art:**
  - The Jardin Majorelle is out. Its name is held as a trade mark, defended in court as late as
    2018, and Jacques Majorelle died in 1962, so his work is still in copyright.
  - Tafraoute's painted rocks (Jean Vérame, 1984) and Asilah's murals are out.
- **No disputed territory:** Western Sahara (Dakhla, Laayoune) is left out.
- **No film tie-ins:** Ouarzazate's film studios are out. Aït Benhaddou is shown as itself,
  with nothing from the films made there.
- **No people,** and no performing animals: Jemaa el-Fnaa, the snake charmers and the monkey
  handlers are out.
- **The goats in argan trees are shown as a grove, never a roadside stop.** Goats do climb
  argan trees to browse in the Souss, but many roadside trees are staged, with goats placed or
  tethered there for tips (reported 2019). So the postcard names the Souss, not a stop.

**Library look-alikes.** These postcards take the family of an everyday picture like them,
so the two never share a board:
- The argan goats take *The Argan Tree*.
- Essaouira takes *Painted Boats*.
- Aït Benhaddou takes *Kasbah*.
- The Todra Gorge takes *Canyon River*.
- El Badi's storks take *Stork*.
- Fes's donkey takes *Donkey*.
- Erg Chebbi takes *Dunes at Sundown*'s family (warm-dusk), as the Dune du Pilat did.
- The Menara takes *Pavilion*.
- Paradise Valley takes *Oasis*.
- The Bahia Palace takes *Riad Courtyard*'s family (jewel-tones).

**Earlier postcards it avoids repeating.**
- Waterfalls (five already), so Ouzoud is a reserve.
- Arches (four already), so Legzira and Akchour's God's Bridge are left out.
- Hoi An's lanterns are coming in trip 15, so Marrakech's lantern souk is left out.

## Left out, and why

- **The Hassan II Mosque, the Koutoubia, the Kairaouine, Moulay Idriss, the zaouias and the
  Hassan Tower:** religious.
- **The royal palaces, portraits, crowns and flags:** royal.
- **The Jardin Majorelle:** trade mark and copyright.
- **Tafraoute's painted rocks and Asilah's murals:** modern art.
- **Western Sahara:** disputed territory.
- **Ouarzazate's film studios:** film tie-ins.
- **Jemaa el-Fnaa, the snake charmers and the monkey handlers:** people, and performing animals.
- **Legzira and Akchour's arches, and the lantern souk:** too like earlier postcards, or like
  trip 15's.
