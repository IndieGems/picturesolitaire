# Argentina: candidate destinations

Trip 7, boards 91–105 (creator, 2026-10-27; slots by Levels, `docs/difficulty-curve.md`
"Trips 7 and 8"). Each board has one real place, shown as a postcard the size of the whole
board. A 2×2 part of it, the **crop**, plays on the board. How postcards are made is in
`docs/picture-direction.md` ("Destinations").

**Shapes.**
- Most boards are 4×5, with a tall 3:5 postcard.
- 95 and 100 are 5×5, with a 3:4 postcard.
- 96 is the trip's all-long board: a 4×4 postcard that plays as a tall two-card strip.

**Tags.** *famous* is a place everyone knows. *hidden gem* is a place few players will
know but they'll want to visit. *silly* is a delightful oddity. The mix is 8 famous, 4
gems and 3 silly.

**Argentina's rights rules, first.**
- **No freedom of panorama in the law** (Ley 11.723). Buildings are accepted as free to
  picture, but sculptures, murals and other artworks are not. So La Boca is shown without
  its murals and statues, and the trip leans on landscapes, wildlife and old engineering.
- **National parks** (Iguazú, Los Glaciares, Tierra del Fuego, Arrayanes) need a permit to
  film or photograph commercially on site (APN, 2018). A generated postcard involves no shoot
  in the park, so the rule doesn't apply. Their names and landscapes are free.
- **No writing:** signs, boat names and the End of the World Post Office's famous sign are
  all text, so none are shown.

**Library look-alikes.** These postcards take the family of an everyday picture like them,
so the two never share a board:
- Iguazú takes *Ribbon Falls*.
- Caminito takes *Pastel Row*'s family.
- The capybaras take *Capybara*.
- Les Éclaireurs takes *Cliff Lighthouse*.
- La Trochita takes *Steam Train*.
- Salinas Grandes takes the salt flats' family (*Salt Flats*, *First Sprout*).
- La Polvorilla takes *Viaduct*.
- The whale takes *Whale*.
- The penguins take *Penguin*.
- La Payunia takes *Volcano*.
- Perito Moreno takes *Iceberg*.

## Left out, and why

- **The Floralis Genérica, the Obelisco and La Boca's murals and statues.** Modern
  artworks, with no freedom of panorama to cover them (the Obelisco is also a monument
  with lettering).
- **The End of the World Post Office.** Its sign is the point of it.
- **Piedra Movediza.** Today's rock is a 2007 replica.
- **Hornocal and Cerro Torre.** Too like Purmamarca and Fitz Roy.
