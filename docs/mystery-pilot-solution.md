# Murder on the Melusine: the solution

**Professor Henri Duval.** The proof is picture 14, the passenger's photo.

1. **The killer was in the cabin at 10:25.** Brandt, alive, took a tray for two,
   and his guest was already inside (9). The poison was the doctor's drops in
   that pot (8).
2. **The watch was staged.** It reads 9:45, when Brandt was still alive (7, 9).
   Henri set it to a time when he sat in the front row in front of everyone.
3. **At 10:25 everyone else was elsewhere.** Clara was on stage (14), the doctor
   was at a seasick passenger's bunk (11), and Margit was in a tavern ashore (12).
4. **Henri lied.** He says he sat in the front row all concert (5), but at 10:25
   his chair, marked by his scarf, is empty (14).
5. **The motive was forgery.** The waltz is in fresh green ink (13), and Henri
   writes in green (5). The experts in Vienna would have exposed him the next day
   (1).

**Red herrings.** Clara would lose everything under the new will (10). The
doctor's drops were the poison (3, 8), and he writes in green too. Margit
threatened to get the waltz (4).

**Accepted answers.** The culprit is Henri. The photo (14) earns the gold stamp.
The tray (9) or the scrap (13) earns the win.
