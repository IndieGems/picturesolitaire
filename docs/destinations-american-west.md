# American West: candidate destinations

Trip 4, boards 46–60 (creator, 2026-10-26). Each board has one real place, shown as a
postcard the size of the whole board. A 2×2 part of it, the **crop**, plays on the board.
The suggested 15 at the end are made; the creator swaps any after seeing them. How
postcards are made is in `docs/picture-direction.md` ("Destinations"), and the US rights
rules are in `docs/destinations-america.md`.

**Shapes.**
- Boards 46–50 and 52–60 are 4×5, so their postcards are a tall 3:5.
- Board 51 is the trip's all-long board: a 4×4 postcard that plays as a two-card strip.

**Tags.** *famous* is a place everyone knows. *hidden gem* is a place few players will
know but they'll want to visit. *silly* is a roadside oddity. The mix is 8 famous, 4 gems
and 3 silly.

**No repeats of trip 1.** The Grand Canyon, Yosemite, Delicate Arch, Grand Prismatic and
the Nā Pali coast are trip 1's. The Golden Gate is out too: it is a library landmark, and
the bridge district licenses its image.

**Library look-alikes.** These postcards take the family of an everyday picture like them,
so the two never share a board:
- Bixby Bridge takes the new *Viaduct*.
- Palouse Falls takes *Ribbon Falls*.
- The Teton barn takes *Red Barn*.

Old Faithful would take *Geyser*, Haystack Rock *Sea Stack*, and the redwoods *Redwoods*.

**Tested.** The 15 suggested places are made and were each checked against Wikipedia or
Commons photos. The other 10 are judged by how well known and how often photographed they
are.

## Left out, and why

- **Monument Valley, Antelope Canyon and Shiprock.** Navajo Nation land, with permits for
  photography. Shiprock is sacred.
- **Havasu Falls.** Havasupai land, reached only by tribal permit.
- **Mount Rushmore and Devils Tower.** A carved sculpture in the sacred Black Hills, and a
  site sacred to many tribes.
- **Mesa Verde and Taos Pueblo.** Ancestral and living Pueblo places: not a game's
  backdrop.
- **The Wave.** Reached only by a permit lottery.
- **The Hollywood and Las Vegas signs.** Lettering, and the Hollywood sign is a trademark.
- **Cadillac Ranch, Salvation Mountain, Seven Magic Mountains.** Modern art, and Salvation
  Mountain is covered in painted text.
- **Cabazon's dinosaurs.** Sculptures from the 1960s–80s, still owned and licensed.
- **Hole N" The Rock and the world's tallest thermometer.** Painted lettering and numbers
  are the point of them.
- **White Sands and the Bonneville Salt Flats.** Every card would look the same.
