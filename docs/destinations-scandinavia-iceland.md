# Scandinavia & Iceland: candidate destinations

Trip 16, boards 226–240 (creator, 2026-11-07). It moved back one trip on 2026-11-07, when East Coast America became trip 11. The trip adds no new art except its postcards
and its stamp: every everyday picture comes from the library. Each board has one real place,
shown as a postcard the size of the whole board. A 2×2 part of it, the **crop**, plays on the
board. How postcards are made is in `docs/picture-direction.md` ("Destinations").

**Shapes** (Levels, `docs/difficulty-curve.md`, "Trips 11-15 (plan)"):
- Most boards are 4×5, with a tall 3:5 postcard.
- 230 and the finale, 240, are 5×5, with a 3:4 postcard. 235 is 4×5.
- 231 is the all-long board: a 4×4 postcard that plays as a two-card strip.

**Tags.** *famous* is a place everyone knows. *hidden gem* is a place few players will know
but they'll want to visit. *silly* is something funny or odd. The mix is 8 famous, 4 gems and
3 silly. Norway has 4 places, Denmark 3, Sweden 3 and Iceland 5.

**The rights rules, first** (checked 2026-11-07).
- **Freedom of panorama differs by country:**
  - Denmark lets buildings be pictured freely, but public artworks only for non-commercial use.
  - Norway lets buildings be pictured, but an artwork only when it is not the main subject.
  - Sweden lets buildings and permanent public art be depicted. Its Supreme Court read that
    narrowly in 2016, for an online database.
  - Iceland's exception is, on Commons' reading, for non-commercial use only.
  - So Iceland's postcards are nature and animals. The others show old buildings, a bridge and
    a folk design, and no sculpture is the subject anywhere.
- **The Little Mermaid is out.** Edvard Eriksen died in 1959, so the statue is in copyright
  until about 2030, and his heirs have taken its pictures to court many times.
- **Harpa is out:** Reykjavík's concert hall allows photography only with its permission.
  Hallgrímskirkja is out as a church, and the Sun Voyager as a modern sculpture.
- **No churches or sacred places:** the stave churches (Borgund, Heddal, Urnes), Búðir's
  black church, Visby's cathedral and church ruins, and Sámi sacred sites are out. No Sámi
  dress, people or reindeer herds.
- **No brands or licensed places:** the ICEHOTEL, the Blue Lagoon, Tivoli, LEGO, IKEA and the
  Flåm Railway are out. No ship, flag or sign shows.
- **No royal insignia:** no crowns, arms or flags. That leaves out Stockholm City Hall, whose
  tower carries the three crowns, and the royal palaces.
- **No film tie-ins:** Kirkjufell and Preikestolen are shown as themselves, with nothing from
  the series and films made there.
- **No danger or tragedy:** Reynisfjara, where sneaker waves have drowned visitors, is left
  out.
- **No people:** every postcard shows a place, an animal or both. Kjeragbolten, whose picture is
  a person standing on the boulder, is left out.

**Library look-alikes.** These postcards take the family of an everyday picture like them,
so the two never share a board:
- Nyhavn takes *Canal House*.
- Lofoten takes *Red Cabin*'s family (red-waterside), as Lunenburg did.
- Kronborg takes *Castle*.
- Møns Klint takes *Sea Stack*.
- Skógafoss takes *Ribbon Falls*.
- The Dala horse takes *Rocking Horse*.
- The Icelandic horses take *Shetland Pony*.
- The Jökulsárlón ice on Diamond Beach takes *Iceberg*.
- The Atlantic Road takes *Viaduct*.
- Dalarna's cottages take *Red Barn*.
- The puffins take *Puffin*.

**Earlier postcards it avoids repeating.**
- The northern lights (Canada), polar bears (Canada) and Strokkur's geyser (Pōhutu, trip 12)
  are left out.
- Of the colourful waterfronts, Nyhavn stays, and Bergen's Bryggen and Stockholm's Gamla Stan
  are reserves.
- Of the fjords, Preikestolen's view stays, and Geirangerfjord is a reserve, after Milford
  Sound.

## Left out, and why

- **The Little Mermaid:** in copyright, with litigious heirs.
- **Harpa, Hallgrímskirkja and the Sun Voyager:** licensed, a church, and modern sculpture.
- **The stave churches, Búðir's church and Visby's churches:** churches.
- **Sámi sacred sites, dress and herds:** sacred, or people's own.
- **The ICEHOTEL, the Blue Lagoon, Tivoli, LEGO, IKEA and the Flåm Railway:** brands.
- **Stockholm City Hall and the royal palaces:** crowns and royal insignia.
- **Reynisfjara:** its waves have drowned visitors.
- **Kjeragbolten:** its picture needs a person.
- **The northern lights, polar bears and Strokkur:** earlier postcards have them.
