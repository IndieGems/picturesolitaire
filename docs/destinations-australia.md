# Australia: candidate destinations

Trip 8, boards 106–120 (creator, 2026-10-27; slots by Levels, `docs/difficulty-curve.md`
"Trips 7 and 8"). New Zealand is the reserve if Australia runs short. Each board has one real
place, shown as a postcard the size of the whole board. A 2×2 part of it, the **crop**, plays
on the board. How postcards are made is in `docs/picture-direction.md` ("Destinations").

**Shapes.**
- Most boards are 4×5, with a tall 3:5 postcard.
- 110 is 5×5 and 120, the finale, is 6×6, both with a 3:4 postcard.
- 111 is the trip's all-long board: a 4×4 postcard that plays as a wide two-card strip.

**Tags.** *famous* is a place everyone knows. *hidden gem* is a place few players will know
but they'll want to visit. *silly* is a roadside oddity. The mix is 8 famous, 4 gems and 3
silly.

**Australia's rights rules, first.**
- **Freedom of panorama covers buildings and structures, and sculptures permanently in public
  places** (Copyright Act 1968, ss 65–66). It does not cover murals or other flat art.
- **No Uluru or Kata Tjuta.** They are sacred to the Anangu, and Parks Australia restricts
  commercial images of them.
- **No Sydney Opera House.** Its image is licensed as a trade mark.
- **The Three Sisters are out too.** They carry a Gundungurra and Dharug Dreaming story.
- **The Harbour Bridge is free to picture.**
  - Checked 2026-10-27: Transport for NSW's registered trade mark is the name *BridgeClimb*.
    I found no licence on the bridge's own image. Australian law lets anyone picture a
    structure, and Commons hosts many photos of it.
  - So the bridge is shown by day, with no BridgeClimb climbers, no fireworks, no lettering
    and no Opera House in frame. The Opera House's own rules ask for that too: it is not to
    dominate a harbour scene.
- **The Big Banana is free to picture.**
  - A 1964 building, which freedom of panorama covers.
  - I found no registered mark on its image in IP Australia's register.
  - Its sign is kept out.
- **No writing:** signs, flags and the painted designs of the bathing boxes (some are flags or
  artworks) are kept out. The boxes are shown in plain colours and stripes.

**Library look-alikes.** These postcards take the family of an everyday picture like them,
so the two never share a board:
- The Twelve Apostles take *Sea Stack*.
- The Brighton boxes take *Beach Huts*.
- Cape Hillsborough takes *Kangaroo*.
- Whitehaven takes *Sandbar*.
- The Pinnacles take *Standing Stones*.
- The quokka takes *Quokka*.
- Cape Tribulation takes *Rainforest Canopy*.
- The Big Banana takes *Bananas*.
- Cradle Mountain takes *Rowboat Lake*.
- Heart Reef takes *Coral Reef*.

## Left out, and why

- **Uluru and Kata Tjuta.** Sacred to the Anangu, and commercial images are restricted.
- **The Sydney Opera House.** Its image is licensed as a trade mark.
- **The Three Sisters.** A Dreaming story of the Gundungurra and Dharug peoples.
- **Lake Hillier.** Too like Hutt Lagoon.
