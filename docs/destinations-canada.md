# Canada: candidate destinations

Trip 6, boards 76–90 (creator, 2026-10-26). Each board has one real place, shown as a
postcard the size of the whole board. A 2×2 part of it, the **crop**, plays on the board.
The suggested 15 at the end are the ones made; the creator can swap any after seeing them.
How postcards are made is in `docs/picture-direction.md` ("Destinations").

**Shapes.**
- Boards 76–80 and 82–90 are 4×5, so their postcards are a tall 3:5.
- Board 81 is the trip's all-long board: a 4×4 postcard that plays as a two-card strip.

**Tags.** *famous* is a place everyone knows. *hidden gem* is a place few players will
know but they'll want to visit. *silly* is a roadside oddity. The mix is 8 famous, 4 gems
and 3 silly.

**Canada's rights rules, first.**
- **Freedom of panorama covers buildings, and sculptures permanently in a public place.**
  So the giant roadside statues are fine. Murals and other flat art are not covered.
- **The CN Tower is out.** Canada Lands holds official marks on its image and licenses
  commercial use.
- **No Indigenous sacred or restricted sites.**
- **No trademarks or writing:** no signs, logos, shop names, or the lettering on the Big
  Nickel.

**Library look-alikes.** These postcards take the family of an everyday picture like them,
so the two never share a board:
- Moraine Lake takes *Rowboat Lake*.
- Abraham Lake takes *Frozen Bubble*.
- The Yellowknife aurora takes *Aurora*.
- Niagara takes *Ribbon Falls*.
- Churchill takes *Polar Bear*.
- Old Quebec takes *Castle*.
- Percé and Hopewell take *Sea Stack*.
- The gannets take *Puffin*.
- Peggy's Cove takes *Cliff Lighthouse*.
- Lunenburg takes *Red Waterside* (the boathouse, red cabin and fishing shack).
- The Cabot Trail takes *Maple Path*'s autumn colours.
- Twillingate takes *Iceberg*.

## Left out, and why

- **The CN Tower.** Canada Lands licenses its image.
- **SGang Gwaay's poles (Haida Gwaii).** A Haida heritage site, visited only with the
  Haida Watchmen.
- **Kliluk, the Spotted Lake.** Sacred to the Syilx Okanagan Nation, and fenced off.
- **Áísínai'pi (Writing-on-Stone).** A Blackfoot sacred site with rock art.
- **The Big Nickel, Sudbury.** Fine to picture, but the coin's lettering is the point of
  it, and the game shows no writing.
- **Anne of Green Gables' house.** The name and character are trademarks.
- **Toronto's Graffiti Alley and Montreal's murals.** Modern flat art, not covered by
  freedom of panorama.
