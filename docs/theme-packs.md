# Themed picture packs — plan (draft for the creator)

Packs are extra sets of pictures with their own boards and album, sold for
Gems. The free game (campaign, challenge route, daily puzzle) stays on the
base library, and every pack board plays by the same rules.

## How a pack fits into the game

- **Home gets a Packs shelf.** Each pack shows its cover, its name, how many
  boards and pictures it has, and the player's progress through it.
- **Free taste.** The first 3 boards of every pack are free to play. The rest
  unlock when the pack is bought. Buying is one tap, at a Gem price; the
  Arcade handles the purchase.
- **Each pack is its own short campaign**, so it never blocks the main game.
  A pack has 20 boards: 16 relaxed boards on a quicker version of the
  campaign's curve, then 4 "pack finale" boards with a par, in the style of
  the challenge route.
- **Each pack fills its own album.** It sits beside the base albums and has
  the same view and zoom.
- **Optional matching table.** A pack can come with a themed table, such as a
  starship console for Sci-Fi. Tables are cosmetics, so they can also be sold
  on their own for Coins. That gives Coins something to buy.
- **The daily puzzle and the challenge route stay on base pictures**, so
  everyone's daily is the same and free.
- **Pack boards pay Coins like campaign boards**, once each, after the server
  has checked the win. The server also checks that the player owns the pack
  before it counts a non-free board.

## How big a pack is

What the campaign does today: a board shows about **6 pictures** (28 cards),
and across 30 boards each of the 40 pictures appears about **4–5 times**.

| | Boards | New pictures | Look-alike sets | Times each picture appears |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard pack (recommended)** | 20 | **32** | 8 sets of 4 | about 3–4 |
| Mini pack | 10 | 16 | 4 sets of 4 | about 3–4 |
| Deluxe pack | 30 | 48 | 12 sets of 4 | about 3–4 |

Why 32 pictures for 20 boards: it keeps each picture fresher than the base
campaign, which matters when someone has paid for it. It also gives each pack
8 look-alike sets, and those sets are what make the puzzle.

A standard pack takes about 1–1½ hours to play (3–4 minutes a board), plus
completing its album.

## Price ideas (your call)

The Arcade's rules: Gems are priced like hit puzzle games, at 1 Gem = 1 US
cent, on round prices. Coins may only buy cosmetics, so **packs are sold for
Gems, never Coins.** Jigsaw-style apps sell packs for about $2–$5.

| | Gems | About |
|---|---|---|
| Mini pack | 200 | $2 |
| Standard pack | 300 | $3 |
| Deluxe pack | 500 | $5 |
| Bundle of 3 standard packs | 800 | $8 |
| A themed table on its own | 1,000–1,500 **Coins** | earned by playing |

## Themes

Every theme needs **look-alike sets inside it**: pictures that share colours
and textures on purpose. Otherwise the pack is too easy. Some example sets
for each theme are below.

| Theme | Example look-alike sets | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Animals** (cozy & wild) | ginger fur (fox, tabby cat, red panda, autumn deer); black & white (panda, zebra, penguins, collie); snowy (arctic fox, owl, seal pup, white horse) | Broadest appeal; likely best seller |
| **Fantasy** | enchanted forests; castles at dusk; dragons in sunset skies; fairy cottages; wizard libraries | Strong painted-illustration fit |
| **Sci-Fi** | neon city streets; space stations; alien sunsets; retro robots; nebula skies | What you asked for; good table tie-in (starship console) |
| **Winter Holidays** | Christmas markets; snowy villages; fireside interiors; ornaments & lights | Matches our snowy table; good for December |
| **Under the Sea** | coral reefs; jellyfish glow; whales & blue water; sunken ships | Rich colour, all ages |
| **Space & Astronomy** | planets; galaxies; aurora skies; observatories | Photographic style, distinct from Sci-Fi |
| **Cozy Interiors** | reading nooks; cafés; kitchens; candlelit rooms | Fits the game's calm mood |
| **Halloween & Harvest** | pumpkins; misty forests; spooky-cute houses; autumn feasts | Seasonal (October) |
| **Desserts & Patisserie** | pastel macarons; chocolate; berry tarts; bakery windows | Very look-alike-friendly |
| **Gardens of the World** | English cottage gardens; Japanese gardens; tulip fields; greenhouses | Pairs with the flower album |
| **Art Through the Ages** | impressionist, Japanese-woodblock, art-nouveau and golden-age-style paintings (new works, no copies) | Lets the painting side shine |
| **Dinosaurs** | jungle scenes; volcano skies; fossils & museums | Kids and families |
| **Steampunk & Clockwork** | brass machines; airships; workshops | Possible cross-promotion with Gearwright |

**Suggested launch lineup:** Animals, Fantasy and Winter Holidays (it lines
up with the season). Then one new pack every 2–4 weeks: Sci-Fi, Under the
Sea, Cozy Interiors…

**Content rules for every pack:** all ages; no brands, film or game
characters, real people or real artworks; no "in the style of" any living
artist.

## What one pack costs to make

- **32 pictures:** about $1.50–$2 of image generation, judging by the first
  60. You review them in the new **Picture Library** editor and flag any you
  don't like, and those get replaced.
- **20 boards:** made and proven winnable automatically, with the same checks
  as the campaign. The win-checker learns the new boards.
- **Cover art, album, optional table** (about $0.30) and store copy.
- **Team time:** roughly a day per pack once the shelf is built. Building the
  shelf the first time (shop, ownership, free taste, pack routes) is a few
  days.

## The base library

You said we'll need many more pictures. Separately from packs, the free
library could grow 20 pictures at a time, towards about 120 pictures and 100
campaign boards. Each batch is reviewed in the Picture Library, and each adds
about 20 boards. Batch 1 (40 → 60 pictures, boards 31–50) is being made now.

## Decisions for you

1. Pack size: standard **20 boards / 32 pictures**, or another size?
2. Prices: 300 Gems a pack, 800 for three?
3. Free taste: **3 boards** of each pack?
4. Does a pack include its matching table, or are tables sold separately for Coins (or both)?
5. Which **3 themes** first? (Suggested: Animals, Fantasy, Winter Holidays.)
